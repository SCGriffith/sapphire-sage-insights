Allegheny County Dog Licenses

I cleaned the 2025 county dog license data and built a dashboard for the county Treasurer's office showing when annual licenses are processed and where dog owners live. The key finding: about 71% of annual licenses are processed in December and January.

About

Hi, I'm Sheila.

I've spent years in business operations and client relations, where I saw how quickly messy data leads to the wrong decisions: sales recorded in the wrong categories, reports nobody quite trusted, and good ideas that were hard to prove with numbers.

Today I pair that hands-on experience with Excel, SQL, and dashboards to help small business owners get clear, reliable answers from their own information. My client-relations background shapes how I work: I listen first, then turn the numbers into recommendations that fit your business and your customers.
